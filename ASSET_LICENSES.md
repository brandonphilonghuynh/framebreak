# Asset provenance — FRAMEBREAK: Solstice Circuit

## Original generated artwork

The following project assets were generated with Codex's **built-in image generation tool** for this game on September 23, 2026. No fallback CLI, personal API key, stock library or downloaded game art was used.

| Final project path | Content | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public/art/solstice-gardens.png` | Sunlit floating garden city | Hub and main arena backdrop |
| `public/art/aurora-spires.png` | Futuristic orbital solar city | Aurora arena; tinted for Helios Foundry |
| `public/art/solstice-crew.png` | Six portraits in a 3 × 2 atlas | Pilot cards, HUD and results |
| `public/art/solstice-bosses.png` | Four portraits in a 2 × 2 atlas | Boss contracts, HUD and results |

The user's supplied images guided atmosphere and composition. Their pixels, logos, interface text and depicted characters are not bundled in the game. Generated PNGs are retained as delivered. The game selects atlas cells with CSS; environment tints are applied during rendering.

These are generated project assets, not third-party assets with an asserted stock/Creative Commons license. No claim is made that generated artwork is exclusive or eligible for copyright everywhere.

## Generation briefs

These summarize the production prompt set used in built-in generation mode:

1. **Solstice Gardens — stylized concept / environment:** An original wide, premium game illustration of a hopeful futuristic solarpunk floating city. Ivory towers and gold solar architecture, lush green hanging gardens, waterfalls, bridges, blue sky and warm sunlight. Calm open central composition suitable behind a fighting arena and menu. No lettering, interface, logos, combatants or foreground gameplay platforms.
2. **Solstice Crew — stylized concept / portrait atlas:** Six original polished painted bust portraits in a precise equal 3-column × 2-row grid, consistent framing and lighting with dark teal backgrounds, no dividers, labels or text. Top row: Vector, brown-skinned curly-haired pilot with goggles and green/gold clothing; Rook, dark-skinned bearded heavy guardian in ivory/green armor; Nyx, brown-skinned woman with silver-violet hair and a cloak. Bottom row: Ember, tan red-haired solar-wing fighter; Solis, dark-skinned white-loc solar guardian with a gold halo; Astra, olive-skinned androgynous navy-haired pilot in cyan/white. Futuristic solar materials and distinct silhouettes.
3. **Aurora Spires — stylized concept / environment:** An original wide futuristic floating garden city in orbit, ivory structures, gold solar collectors, floating islands, hanging greenery, waterfalls, clear blue light and a large planet overhead. Premium readable game background with room for combat; no UI, lettering, existing game characters or foreground gameplay platforms.
4. **Solar Bosses — stylized concept / portrait atlas:** One equal 2 × 2 grid of original nonhuman solar boss portraits, consistent dark teal background, detailed ivory/gold futuristic materials, no labels or UI. Top left Verdant Warden: moss-covered ivory garden golem, green luminous eyes and branches. Top right Vesper: faceless violet/indigo prism entity. Bottom left Heliarch: massive ivory furnace sun king with orange/gold radiance. Bottom right Eclipse: black/teal/white/gold seraph with six wings and a dark star. No human faces.

## Original code, graphics and audio

Fighter rigs, weapons, robots, moving platforms, hazard markers, UI icons, effects and animation are original procedural code created for FRAMEBREAK. The combat rigs are separate from the painted portrait atlases. Optional effects and soundtrack use original Web Audio oscillator sequences, with no sampled recordings.

System Arial/Helvetica/monospace/sans-serif fonts are referenced, not distributed. No Nintendo artwork, characters, branding or audio is included.
